Quick rules

🌱 Farm where yield bonus is useful+
Choose crops or animals in cities where the island or local yield bonus improves the output you actually need for your production loop.
🔨 Refine in resource-bonus city+
Move raw resources to the Royal City with the matching refining bonus before refining, when the extra return is worth the transport.
⚒ Craft in item-bonus city+
Craft each item in the city that gives that item category a bonus, then compare the expected sale price against materials, fees and transport.
💰 Sell where margin survives tax + transport+
Do not choose a destination from the highest price alone. Subtract market fees, taxes, transport and the risk of stale market data first.
